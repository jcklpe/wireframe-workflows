# Change Showroom deliberately with provenance
Agents must follow the complete [change showroom deliberately with provenance design agreement](../../policies/showroom.md) when its scope applies. Its requirements, exceptions, ownership boundaries and qualifications remain intact. The agreement is shared design guidance; this policy establishes the agent obligation to apply it, without claiming authority over people.

Apply the relevant wireframing skill for the task procedure. Neither this policy nor a skill grants permission to change a design file, another owner's work, tools or external systems. Preserve the authorization and ownership limits in the agreement and the actual project instructions. Report a substantive conflict for human resolution rather than silently changing the agreement or treating a successful structural check as acceptance.
